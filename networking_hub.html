Strategy — Why Target Incoming Analysts?
Incoming analysts (starting July / August) are the most accessible people at any firm. They haven't built walls yet. They're eager to meet people in the same boat. And crucially — a warm "I know that analyst" moment during a superday or info call is worth 10 cold emails to a VP. Message them before recruiting season, so by the time it starts you're already a name they recognize.
How to find them on LinkedIn
1. Search [Firm name] analyst 2025 or [Firm] analyst class of 2025
2. Go to the firm's LinkedIn page → People → filter "Past 90 days" (recently joined)
3. Check the firm's own LinkedIn posts — they often post "Welcome to our incoming class!"
4. Filter your 2nd-degree connections by company — new grads at target firms are often just 2 hops away
